Title: Electronic Media Use and Sleep Quality: Meta-Analysis

Source:
Han, X. et al. (2024)

Summary:
This meta-analysis examines the relationship between electronic media use, including social media and smartphones, and sleep quality. The results show a consistent negative relationship between media use and sleep, with problematic use having a stronger effect.

Core Ideas:
- Electronic media use significantly reduces sleep quality
- Problematic use has stronger negative effects than general use
- Sleep disruption increases with increased usage intensity

Practical Strategies:
- Reduce overall screen time
- Avoid problematic or compulsive use patterns
- Prioritize sleep over late-night media use
- Establish structured bedtime routines

Triggers:
- Excessive screen time
- Problematic or compulsive usage
- Nighttime device use
- Lack of routine
