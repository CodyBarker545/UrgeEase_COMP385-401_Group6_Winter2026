Title: Digital Addiction and Sleep Disorders

Source:
Reference Link: https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19116910
Dresp-Langley, B. (2022)

Summary:
This paper explores the relationship between digital addiction, sleep disorders, and mental health. It highlights that insomnia and sleep disruption are not only consequences of addiction but can also act as triggers that reinforce addictive behaviors.

Core Ideas:
- Sleep disorders and addiction are strongly interconnected
- Insomnia can act as both a trigger and a consequence of addiction
- Poor sleep increases emotional vulnerability and compulsive behavior
- Addiction, sleep problems, and depression form a reinforcing cycle

Practical Strategies:
- Improve sleep hygiene (consistent sleep schedule)
- Reduce digital exposure before bedtime
- Address emotional triggers contributing to poor sleep
- Develop routines that separate technology use from sleep

Triggers:
- Insomnia
- Late-night device use
- Emotional distress (anxiety, depression)
- Irregular sleep schedules
