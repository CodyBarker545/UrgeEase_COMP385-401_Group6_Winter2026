Title: The Association Between Smartphone Addiction and Sleep Quality

Source:
Sohn, S. Y. et al. (2021)

Summary:
This study investigates the relationship between smartphone addiction and sleep quality in young adults. Results show a strong association between excessive smartphone use and poor sleep outcomes. Individuals with higher levels of smartphone addiction are significantly more likely to experience sleep disturbances.

Core Ideas:
- Smartphone addiction is strongly associated with poor sleep quality
- Addictive use leads to disrupted sleep patterns and reduced rest
- Behavioral overuse contributes to insomnia and fatigue
- Sleep problems worsen addiction cycles

Practical Strategies:
- Reduce screen time before sleep (especially 1–2 hours before bed)
- Avoid phone use in bed
- Set consistent sleep and wake times
- Limit nighttime notifications
- Establish a structured bedtime routine

Triggers:
- Nighttime phone use
- Habitual late-night scrolling
- Emotional dependence on devices
- Lack of structured sleep routine

- 68.7% of addicted users had poor sleep quality compared to lower rates in non-addicted users :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
